Flying Vehicle Overhaul 0.8 — Early WIP

Hover vehicles with gears, terrain following and autopilot. Summon your vehicle, have it follow or cover you, send it along scanner routes, or let a follower drive while you ride as a passenger. NPCs can use compatible vehicles for travel and combat.

What it does

  • Hover flight: four gears, boost, climb, descend and terrain following. Standard hover works with Physical VTOL switched off.
  • Physical flight — experimental: thrust, torque, native mass and inertia produce momentum and banking. Flight assist stabilizes drift and rotation; camera roll and an attitude instrument show pitch and bank. Separate player and AI settings, both off by default.
  • Autopilot and routes: summon, follow, cover, guard and scanner destinations. Chain stops, retry a held trip or skip a stop. Navigation considers terrain, walls, ceilings, people, vehicles, weather and darkness.
  • Followers and passengers: request a follower driver, ride with an NPC or take the wheel. Escorts can defend, intercept, scout and rescue; a following vehicle can attempt to catch you from below.
  • Combat and faction reactions: vehicle manoeuvres, squad roles, morale, attacks and retreats. Set Defend / Attack / Hold fire, or focus a hostile person or hostile-driven vehicle. Ownership and allegiance govern vehicle use and friendly-fire protection.
  • Damage and wrecks: shots and collisions damage occupied and empty vehicles, can injure occupants and can leave wrecks. Vehicle health stays with the chassis through driver changes.
  • NPC activity: compatible vehicles can support trips, shared rides, patrols and supply runs when suitable NPCs and vehicles are available.
  • Ground navigation — experimental: optional ground-first route planning around terrain hazards and buildings, with native driving for occupied vehicles. Off by default.
  • Controls and HUD: separate keyboard/controller bindings with context, tap, hold and double tap. Move and resize HUD groups together or adjust individual status-card elements, route text, escort rows and action slots within them.

New in 0.8

  • FOMOD installer: independent choices for the two content addons, plus individual feature switches grouped under Flight and Navigation, NPCs and Combat, HUD, and Parked Vehicles.
  • Settings import: choose features during installation or Keep existing settings. Choices apply on the next launch; unchanged installer choices preserve later in-game adjustments. Bindings and saved HUD layouts remain intact.
  • Installer fixes: corrected XML parsing and the false Starfield.esm prerequisite check. Addon choices check their required masters. Placement recording and detailed logging are not installer choices.
  • HUD editing: individual movement and sizing inside groups; fixed the fourth escort row and duplicate information-panel movement. My current HUD arrangement is now the built-in default; existing saved layouts take priority.
  • Navigation and combat: added experimental ground planning/native driving, off by default, and focus fire against hostile-driven vehicles.
  • Downloads: separate main and optional-addon packages, with an alternative all-in-one FOMOD.

Requirements

  • Steam Starfield 1.16.244 and its matching Starfield Script Extender (SFSE). Launch through sfse_loader.exe. Game Pass and Microsoft Store are not supported.
  • Core-game vehicle content is sufficient for the main mod and Extra Vehicles addon. Carfield and Parked Vehicles are optional; only the Parked Vehicles conversion patch requires Parked Vehicles and its listed vehicle-content masters.

Downloads and included files

  • FlyingVehicleOverhaul-0.8.zip — main: FlyingVehicleOverhaul.dll provides flight, navigation, NPC vehicle systems, damage, controls, settings and HUD. FVO_NPCPilots.esm supplies NPC walking and boarding packages. Includes an installation guide.
  • FlyingVehicleOverhaul-0.8-ExtraVehicles.zip — optional: FVO_CityPlacements.esm adds parked vehicles around New Atlantis using core-game vehicle content. No separate vehicle mod required. Includes its guide.
  • FlyingVehicleOverhaul-0.8-ParkedVehiclesPatch.zip — optional: FVO_ParkedVehiclesPatch.esm converts the Parked Vehicles mod's static props into drivable vehicles. Requires that mod and all masters listed in the included guide; load after them. Do not enable alongside the older Vehicles - Real.esm conversion patch.
  • FlyingVehicleOverhaul-0.8-FOMOD.zip — all-in-one alternative: the same main files and both optional addons, with dependency checks and grouped feature setup for Vortex/MO2. Both addons start unchecked. Use this instead of the separate ZIPs.

Installation and updating

  • Mod manager: install the FOMOD, or install the main ZIP plus whichever optional addon ZIPs you want.
  • Manual: extract the main ZIP and wanted addon ZIPs into Starfield\Data. The DLL belongs in Data\SFSE\Plugins. Do not extract the entire FOMOD archive into Data.
  • Enable plugins: FVO_NPCPilots.esm and whichever optional addon ESMs you installed. Launch through SFSE; restart after replacing the DLL.
  • Updating: replace the previous FVO installation. Remove SeamlessVehicle.dll if upgrading from that older filename; leaving both DLLs installed loads both copies. An unchecked addon does not remove a copy installed through another mod entry or manually.

Settings and defaults

  • Feature settings: Settings > Gameplay Options > Flying Vehicle Overhaul. Bindings: Settings > Controls.
  • On by default: NPC vehicle use, full combat AI, hover-or-drive behavior, damage and wrecks, health bars, status card, crash witnesses, parked-vehicle support and flight assist.
  • Off by default: free roll, player Physical VTOL, AI Physical VTOL and experimental ground planning/native driving. Placement recording and detailed logging also default off. Existing saved settings can override defaults.
  • Parked-vehicle support does not install either optional content addon.

Configuration folder: Documents\My Games\Starfield\SFSE\Logs\FlyingVehicleOverhaul

  • settings.ini: feature settings. bindings.ini: keyboard/controller bindings. hoverui.ini: HUD positions and sizes. FlyingVehicleOverhaul.log is one folder above these files.

Keyboard and mouse defaults

  • Driving: W/S accelerate/brake; A/D turn or roll; mouse looks/aims. V hover/land; Q/Z climb/descend; Alt tap next gear, hold descend. Shift boosts; double tap locks throttle in flight. Fresh boost or brake releases the lock.
  • Vehicle commands: T with scanner closed toggles Auto; movement takes control back. E exits, landing first while hovering. B toggles flight assist; K cycles stance; right bracket cycles your vehicle's navigation profile; J shows all hints.
  • On foot: N tap summon, hold recall vehicle and escorts. U tap follow/cover, hold request a follower driver. Facing an occupied vehicle, Activate tap boards as passenger; hold takes the driver's seat.
  • Passenger: G tap nearby boards; as passenger, tap exits and hold takes the wheel. Look to steer and W/S adjust speed when a taxi destination is not supplying guidance. F9 cycles escort navigation profiles.
  • Scanner: T tap destination/queued stop, double tap clear route, hold guard/focus an aimed hostile. H focus fire; O clear focus; semicolon retry; left bracket skip stop.

Controller defaults

Xbox / PlayStation names. Hold: 0.5 seconds; double tap: within 0.3 seconds. Custom bindings override these defaults.

  • Driving: left stick throttle/brake/turn/roll; right stick look. L3 hover/land; A/Cross tap gear, hold descend; Y/Triangle climb; X/Square descend. RB/R1 boost, double tap throttle lock. B/Circle exits with landing first. RT/R2 fires; LT/L2 aims.
  • Vehicle commands: D-pad Left tap Auto, hold navigation profile; Up recalls escorts; Right cycles stance; Down toggles flight assist in flight. R3 tap shows all hints.
  • Scanner orders: B/Circle tap summon, hold recall escorts too. L3 tap follow/cover, hold follower driver, double tap focus fire. Y/Triangle tap destination/stop, hold guard/focus, double tap clear route.
  • Scanner route actions: D-pad Left tap retry, hold navigation profile, double tap clear focus. Right tap skip, hold ride along, double tap escort profiles. View/Create tap stance, hold all hints, double tap HUD editor.
  • Passenger: A/Cross tap exit, hold take the wheel. Right stick steers; left stick up/down changes speed when a taxi destination is not supplying guidance.

HUD editor

  • Keyboard: F7 opens/saves/closes; Page Up/Down selects; arrows move; Home/End adjust supported spacing; +/- resize; Delete resets the selection; Ctrl increases movement steps.
  • Controller: scanner View/Create double tap opens; LB/RB selects; left stick moves; D-pad Left/Right adjusts spacing; RT/LT resizes; X/Square resets; L3 increases movement steps; B/Circle saves/closes.
  • Groups and elements: moving a panel carries its arrangement; individual children retain their own offsets and sizes. Foot and vehicle status cards have separate layouts. The enemy vehicle health bar remains attached to the native enemy meter.

Status and known issues

  • Early WIP: basic hover flight and summon/follow have been seen working in game. Recent navigation, combat, escort, controller, HUD and physical-flight changes need further gameplay testing; automated checks do not establish live performance.
  • Vehicle behavior: Physical AI may turn wide or overshoot. Parked vehicles may start partly inside the ground or hop. Atmospheric lift/stalls and save/load persistence of transient vehicle jobs are not included.
  • Performance: Jemison CPU/FPS drops were reported with 0.73.0. The cause remains unisolated; 0.8 is not a confirmed fix. Extra live vehicles can increase CPU/physics load. Compare with both optional addons disabled when reporting performance.

Reporting and compatibility

  • Include your game/mod version, input device, location, optional plugins, command used and scanner/driving/passenger state. Copy Documents\My Games\Starfield\SFSE\Logs\FlyingVehicleOverhaul.log before restarting; include bindings.ini for custom-control problems.
  • Mods changing the same vehicle physics, AI, controls, HUD hooks or placement areas may conflict. Game updates require a compatible build.
