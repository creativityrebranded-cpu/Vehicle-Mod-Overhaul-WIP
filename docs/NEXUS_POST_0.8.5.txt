FVO 0.8.5 - AI navigation, entering and exiting, passengers and your own traffic routes

AI navigation has a new autopilot. It scores the headings around the vehicle, plans short routes round obstacles and recovers from stuck spots by taking the most open direction, including up when flying is allowed. Combat prefers flight and traffic prefers the ground. Climbs to high lanes now work, and arriving traffic sinks slowly onto its spot instead of hovering.

Entering and exiting were reworked. You are no longer seated on top of an NPC driver. Holding to pull a driver out uses the game's own get-up, so they step out instead of hanging in a sitting pose, and you take the real driver's seat. Getting out high up drops you beside the vehicle. A crash while loading saves is fixed.

The passenger system is new. Enter a vehicle someone is driving and you take the passenger seat while their autopilot drives; your movement keys are blocked there. G taps out and holds to take the wheel. Followers take the passenger seats when you drive.

Routes are now yours to make. Traffic flies routes written in plain .ini files in Data\SFSE\Plugins\FVO_Traffic: rings or open and looping paths, exits, landing places and per-world rules. FVO re-reads them every 10 seconds. "Show traffic routes" places probes along them. NewAtlantis.ini is a working example and HOW-TO-MAKE-ROUTES.txt explains every line.

INSTALL / UPDATE
Use FlyingVehicleOverhaul-0.8.5.zip plus any addon ZIPs, or the FOMOD instead. The installer keeps the 0.8 options: New Atlantis City Traffic (now the route traffic DLL and files) and New Atlantis City Placements (ESM) are separate; use either or both. Replace the previous FVO entry, enable FVO_NPCPilots.esm and launch through SFSE for Steam Starfield 1.16.244. Choose "Keep existing gameplay settings" to keep your configuration. Remove the old FVO_CityTraffic.esm if present.

Still WIP: passenger seats, pulling drivers out and route trips have been seen working; parking descent, route probes and follower seating need more testing. Please include your FVO log and, for route problems, your traffic file.
